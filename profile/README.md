# LCC Research Lab

Independent research exploring how artificial intelligence learns, reasons, and acquires knowledge.

Our *Adventures in AI* book series documents reproducible experiments with language models, from evidence and interpretation to internal model measurements.

[AI Model Inspector](https://github.com/LCCResearchLab/ai-model-inspector-public) is our free, open-source scientific instrument for examining model weights and LoRA updates.

Explore our research and publications: [lccresearchlab.com](https://lccresearchlab.com)
